BetterOTG v1.0 — Initial Release:
- 100% FOSS, offline USB OTG microscope, endoscope & UVC inspection camera viewer
- Source-built libusb + libuvc native engine with MJPEG & YUYV support
- Resolution/FPS switcher, 1x–8x pinch zoom, rotation, H/V mirror, Fit/Fill & immersive fullscreen
- Measurement reticle (mm / inch), dual-screen comparison & live UVC hardware controls
- JPEG snapshots, H.264 MP4 recording, date/time watermark, PiP floating window & built-in Gallery
- Keep Screen On, physical USB shutter button support, Settings About & separate Changelog window
